ITEM 2b  does a between-wave change detector survive that spread?
======================================================================================
Per split, fit offset(pi) = b0 + b1*pi over pi in [0.1, 0.3].
Two waves at pi1, pi2 estimated by adjusted count:
  same calibration split  -> b0 cancels; the change is scaled by (1+b1).
  re-drawn split          -> the change also picks up b(2)(pi2)-b(1)(pi1).

Same split: the change is multiplied by (1+b1), so the error is RELATIVE, 2*sd(b1).
Re-drawn:   the change gains an ADDITIVE noise, sd = sqrt(sd(b@pi1)^2 + sd(b@pi2)^2);
            MDC is 2x that, an absolute floor on any detectable change in pi.
